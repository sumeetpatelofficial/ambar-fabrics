01

Header

Announcement bar, then a centred wordmark with category links left and icons right. It floats transparent over the hero.

Sticky. Fades to solid ivory with a hairline over the first 140 px of scroll.

gsap.to(header, { backgroundColor, scrollTrigger: { scrub } })

Logo mark as SVG
02

Hero carousel

Three collection slides — Festive edit, Everyday cotton, Wedding dupattas. Large serif headline, model standing in an arch, a price tag card and two calls to action.

Auto-advances every 6 s with progress lines you can click. On change, the model is revealed bottom-up and the text rises in. Scrolling away drifts the hero up and fades it.

fromTo(clipPath: inset(100%→0)) · stagger on text · scrub yPercent

3 model cut-outs, transparent WebP/PNG, ~1600 px tall, same framing
03

The wardrobe

One model, four outfits. The outfit list on the left, the model in the centre, the outfit's details, swatches, price and fabric link on the right. A measuring tape fills along the bottom.

Section holds still for ~4 screens. Each scroll step drapes the next outfit down over the model (gold scan line at the edge), the list and details swap, the background tint shifts. Snaps to each look so it never stops half-changed.

timeline + scrollTrigger { scrub, snap: 1/(n−1) } · clipPath wipe

4–6 photos of the same AI model in the same pose and framing — only the outfit changes, so the wipe lines up
04

Shop by category

A dark band with seven tall category cards: Fabrics, Ready-made, Unstitched suits, Dupattas, Blouses, Tassels & latkans, Laces & borders — then "All categories".

Vertical scroll moves the rail sideways. Cards lift and straighten as they enter, the textile drifts inside each card, a gold line tracks progress.

to(track, { x: −distance }) · containerAnimation for per-card effects

1 image per category, 4:5 portrait
05

Every angle — 360°

Model on the left, story on the right: Front, Side, Back, then Full turn with "Shop the set" and "Fabric · ₹420 per metre".

The page scrolls normally; the model column sticks and turns 0→360° with scroll progress, with a dial and live degree readout. Text blocks rise in as they arrive.

Mockup: rotationY on a preserve-3d front/back card. With real photos: draw frame = round(progress × 35) of an image sequence on a <canvas>.

A turntable of the AI model: 36–72 frames (or a 360° video exported to frames), WebP, same lighting
06

Looks in motion

A Pinterest-style lookbook: three staggered columns of outfit cards, each linking to the piece or its fabric. A large italic line of craft names sits behind.

Columns move at different speeds (the middle one against the others), photos settle from a slight zoom, the craft names slide sideways.

scrub yPercent per column · xPercent marquee · scale 1.14→1

9–12 lookbook shots, 3:4, varied backgrounds
2.5 m
07

Cut to order

Your "buy only the length you need" promise, shown instead of told: a fabric roll, a measuring tape, and a big length and price readout.

Holds still while the fabric unrolls off the roll along the tape; the length counts up in 0.25 m steps and the price follows (block print cotton at ₹220 per metre).

scrub scaleX + onUpdate → textContent

One long, flat fabric texture (seamless if possible)
08

New arrivals

Your current product cards, quieter: no card borders, a single tag, price with unit, sale price struck through, colour dots, wishlist.

Cards fade up in a stagger as the row arrives; image zooms gently on hover. Bestsellers can reuse the same block.

ScrollTrigger.batch(cards, { onEnter → stagger })

Product photos, 4:5

09

Festive offer

A deep ochre band: a running offer ticker, a huge percentage, a "Shop the offer" button and a tear-off coupon with the code, a copy button and the terms.

Ticker slides sideways with scroll, the number rises out of a mask, the coupon swings in from a tilt and settles. "Copy code" copies to the clipboard and confirms.

scrub xPercent · yPercent mask reveal · rotation + x on ticket

Offer %, code, end date, minimum order
10

Customer reviews

Two rows of quote cards, each with the review, the customer's name and city, and the product they bought.

The rows drift in opposite directions as you scroll past, so the wall of reviews feels alive without autoplaying.

scrub xPercent, opposite sign per row

6–8 real reviews (Google, Instagram, order emails) with permission
